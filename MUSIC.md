# Music

The game uses `assets/circuit-punk-game-menu.mp3`, downloaded from the supplied OpenGameArt page. It is the looping “Circuit Punk Game Menu” track by Eric Matyas / soundimage.org. The page lists it under CC-BY 3.0; attribution is required.

The current calibration is 120 BPM with a zero-second beat offset. The runner, build grid, catapult delays, laser timing, and timetable triggers all derive their spacing from the level’s `music.bpm` field. During a quiz, the track pitch is reduced together with the world speed.
