# Music

The game uses `assets/cyberpunk-menu-music.mp3`, downloaded directly from the supplied OpenGameArt page: https://opengameart.org/content/cyberpunk-menu-music

This is the page’s “Cyberpunk Menu Music” track by ZaninDevelopers. The page provides MP3, OGG, and WAV downloads and lists the track under CC0. The current gameplay calibration is 120 BPM with a zero-second beat offset; runner speed, build spacing, catapult delays, and timetable triggers derive from that value. During a quiz, the track pitch is reduced together with the world speed.

The title screen’s `M` key, or its touch button, cycles through these additional OpenGameArt options:

- “Murder on the Metrorail” by joeBaxterWebb, ISAo, and SRG774 — CC-BY 4.0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/murder-on-the-metrorail-joebaxterwebb-isao-srg774
- “Boss Fight” by AiTechEye — OGA-BY 4.0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/boss-fight-1
- “Funky Chiptune (Looping)” by Eric Matyas — CC-BY 3.0. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/funky-chiptune-looping
